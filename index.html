Privacy Policy — Synaplex

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Synaplex is a puzzle game published by CxT Games Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Synaplex mobile app (Android package com.cxtgamesstudio.synaplex).

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name or email address.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We do not upload it anywhere.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects device and advertising identifiers.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information handled by the app

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Game progress: level reached, stars earned, hints remaining, purchased items, sound and theme settings So the game remembers where you were Stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
Advertising identifier, IP address, device model, OS version, coarse location derived from IP, ad interactions To show and measure ads Collected by Google AdMob, our advertising partner
Purchase receipts To unlock what you bought and to restore it if you reinstall Handled by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation of the purchase, not your card details.
Local reminder notifications To remind you to come back and play Scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Advertising

Synaplex uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads relevant to you and to limit how often you see the same ad. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time from the in-game Settings screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Buying the "Remove Ads" item removes banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain available, because you choose to watch those in exchange for a hint.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card, billing address, or Google account details. Purchases are non-consumable: use Restore Purchases in Settings to recover them on a new device.

Children

Synaplex is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold an account for you, we hold almost no data that identifies you personally. You can:

  • Delete everything we store on your device by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's data in your device settings.
  • Control ad personalisation through the consent screen in the app and through your device's advertising settings.
  • Ask about data held by Google (our ads and billing provider) through your Google account.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have rights under the GDPR to access, correct, and erase personal data, and to object to its processing. If you are a California resident, you have similar rights under the CCPA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. To exercise any of these, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that handle data differently — for example analytics or cloud save — we will update this page and change the date at the top before those features ship.
